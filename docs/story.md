# Story: Servus, Kid

The story bible for the quest line. Every quest text follows the names and beats here. Paracelsus' own story stays in Alchemy's journal; the quests only point to it.

Language: plain, everyday English, short sentences. Alchemy terms (Nigredo, Rubedo, Caput Mortuum) only where Alchemy itself uses them, with a short explanation the first time.

## The people and places

- **Uncle Vinzenz** (Vinzenz Huber, 1931 – ?), the player's great-uncle. Mining engineer at the Berchtesgaden salt mine until he retired, then a tinkerer, collector and, in secret, alchemist. Writes in short, dry sentences, with a soft spot for machines and an old man's jokes. Never married. Signs every letter "Uncle Vinzenz".
- **The player**, who inherits his land. Vinzenz never met them as a grown-up; he calls them "kid", never by name or gender.
- **The farm**: the Huber land in the mountains above Berchtesgaden, in Bavaria. There is no house any more. The house, the workshop and the cellar went down into the mountain the winter Vinzenz disappeared. The player inherits the land and builds everything again.
- **Huber & Co.**, the family trading company, founded in 1521. It carried Berchtesgaden salt to Salzburg and Augsburg. Long gone; Vinzenz wanted to bring it back.
- **Paracelsus' Journal**: family story says Paracelsus stayed with the Hubers on his way to Salzburg and left his journal behind. Vinzenz kept it in the cellar; it went down with the house. The player makes a new copy (Alchemy's own item).
- **The old gnome**: the Old Miner from Alchemy's journal, Paracelsus' friend, who lives in the veins of the mountains. Vinzenz heard him knocking under the farm.

## How the letters work

Vinzenz left a bundle of sealed letters with the notary, each marked "to be opened when ...". Every quest text is one of these letters or a page he copied from his notebook into them. Letters open with "Servus, kid," and end with "Uncle Vinzenz". "Servus" is the Bavarian hello and goodbye; the last letter uses it as goodbye.

## The mystery

Vinzenz disappeared two winters ago. No body, no note, and no house: where it stood there is only a meadow. The letters reveal step by step what happened:

1. He finished the alchemist's work: the red fire burned in his cellar.
2. Alchemy can make everything that exists. He wanted to make something that does not exist yet ("beyond the Stone").
3. He heard knocking under the cellar and dug down after it. The mountain took the house when he went below. He apologises for that early on ("I dug too deep") without explaining.
4. He is not dead. He stayed below with the old gnome, looking after the veins of the mountain. The last letter, found "where the dust trickles from one block", tells the player so and says goodbye.

## The last letter (finale)

> Servus, kid,
>
> if you are reading this, you have made what I could only draw. Good. I knew the farm would be in better hands.
>
> I did not die. I went down after the knocking, and the mountain closed behind me. Sorry about the house. The old one was waiting, the same one who once waited for Paracelsus. There is work down here that no alchemy does: the veins need looking after, and someone has to listen to the mountain. I am slow now, and very happy.
>
> Do not come looking. Leave a pinch of salt in the deepslate now and then, and listen. If it knocks twice, that is me.
>
> The trading company is yours. Keep the books straight.
>
> Servus,
> Uncle Vinzenz
