# At Your Servus

*Servus, kid,*

*if the notary has done his job, you are standing on my land with this letter in your hand, looking for a house. Stop looking. There is none any more. I dug too deep. Sorry about that.*

Your great-uncle **Vinzenz Huber** disappeared two winters ago. No body, no note, and no house: where his farm stood above Berchtesgaden there is only a meadow. All he left you is the land and a bundle of sealed letters with the notary, each marked "to be opened when ...".

Build the farm again, bring the old family trading company **Huber & Co.** back to life, and find out what Uncle Vinzenz went looking for deep inside the mountain.

## A guided path with a story

FTB Quests leads you through eight chapters. Every quest is one of Uncle Vinzenz's letters or a page from his notebook.

1. **Arrival**: a roof, a fire and something to eat
2. **The Works**: steam, ducts and your first warehouse, powered by a coal generator
3. **The Cellar**: Paracelsus' Journal and the four fires of alchemy
4. **The Power Station**: coal is not enough for a trading company
5. **Into the Nether**: tools and armour for behind the burning gate
6. **The Trading House**: Huber & Co. lives again
7. **Uncle Vinzenz's Workshop**: jetpacks, and the spirit that rises
8. **Beyond Alchemy**: what Uncle Vinzenz went looking for

Four side chapters cover exploration and dimensions, Occultism, Apotheosis and mob farms, and building and decor.

## Built around the Servus mods

- **Servus: Foundation**: early-game machines, ducts and steam
- **Servus: Alchemy**: Paracelsus' Journal and the four fires, your path to resource generation
- **Servus: Shuttles**: warehouse automation with shuttles, put-away rules and autocrafting

Other mods fill the gaps: **Powah** for power, **Just Dire Things** for the Nether, **Iron Jetpacks** for flight, and **Re:Avaritia** as the endgame. The top jetpacks and the Infinity Catalyst need what you made in the cellar, the warehouse and the power station.

## Good to know

- Minecraft 26.1.2, NeoForge
- Give the game at least **6 GB** of RAM
- A server pack is available for every version
- Storage and autocrafting are Shuttles' job. There is no AE2 or Refined Storage, on purpose.
